# Airbnb-demand-analysis
This project is part of analysis of Inside Airbnb dataset of several cities done by PSL master's class of 2025-26 and supervised by Professor Christophe Benavent. 
It looks at how parks and trees affect Airbnb supply and demand in Melbourne. By combining Airbnb data with the city’s 2025 tree inventory, we analyzed over 24,000 listings and reviews using text-mining and mapping tools. We found that reviews mentioning green spaces are significantly more positive, and listings that use nature-related language attract 93% more reviews on average. Over 86% of hosts use green keywords to market their properties, with these listings heavily concentrated in park-rich and coastal neighborhoods like Port Phillip and Yarra. Our analysis shows that guests strongly link nature terms with positive feelings like comfort and quiet. These findings show that urban greenery heavily shapes the local short term rental market, adding important evidence to ongoing debates about housing availability and who benefits from public investments in city parks.
